How to order your cards at MB PRINT

Follow these steps to quickly order physical copies of your freshly generated cards.

  1. Prepare your file link:

    After downloading your ready-to-print PDF file, upload it to a cloud storage service (e.g., Google Drive). Right-click the file, share it, and make sure to select "Anyone with the link". Copy the link to your clipboard.

  2. Visit MB PRINT:

    Go to the custom game cards printing page:
    mbprint.pl/en/product/custom-game-cards-printing/

  3. Configure the parameters:

    Set the printing options exactly as shown in the screenshot below:

    • Cards size: 61.5×88 mm (2.42×3.46 in)
    • Number of cards in the deck: Enter the amount of cards (you can easily calculate this by dividing the number of pages in your PDF by 2).
    • Number of ordered decks: Enter how many copies of the deck you want.
    • Paper weight: matt paper 300g/m2
    • Card finishing: No
    • Double-sided printed cards: Double-sided
    • Rounded corners: Checked
  4. Order prints:

    Click the "Order prints" button at the bottom.

  5. Provide your file:

    In the next section, select your cloud drive icon (e.g., Google Drive) and paste the link you copied in Step 1.
